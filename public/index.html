Daniel↗

My work sits where research, business operations, and technology meet.

My background in legal support has shaped how I approach information: follow the source, ask a better question, and make the next step clear. I bring that same attention to delivery workflows, estimating tools, documents, and websites.

I use AI as part of a hands-on process—defining the problem, exploring options, reviewing the result, and refining what people will actually use.
